Rooms & Suites

Rooms & Suites at Mohor Kutir

On the edge of Tagore's Santiniketan, seven room categories share the same hand — cane and sal-wood furniture, hand-block linen, a lotus mark carved into every headboard. Premium Suites open onto private balconies; pool-facing rooms step straight onto the deck; interconnecting Club Rooms and ground-floor Cottages suit families travelling together.

Deluxe Room

The entry category, and still full-sized — garden-facing, with the same cane and sal-wood furniture as the rest.

Premium Suite

The largest of the rooms, with its own balcony and a wall lamp cut in the shape of a Santhal totem.

Club Room

Interconnecting rooms built for families and small groups, on the ground floor throughout.

Premium Suite

The largest of the rooms, and the quietest. A wall-mounted lamp cut in the shape of a Santhal totem throws lattice shadows across the headboard by evening; the sal-wood bed frame is dressed in cream and rust, the resort's own palette.

  • Private balcony, garden facing
  • Cane and sal-wood furniture throughout
  • Rain shower and marble-topped vanity
02

Garden & Pool-Facing Rooms

Superior and Super Deluxe rooms open directly onto the lawns or the pool deck — a curtained sliding door, a small table for morning tea, and the sound of the property's resident sunbirds rather than traffic.

  • Direct lawn or pool-deck access
  • Floral wall art from local artists
  • Twin or king configurations available
03

Cottage & Club Rooms

Ground-floor cottages and interconnecting Club Rooms are built for families and small groups travelling together — two rooms, one shared door, the same swan folded on every bed.

  • Interconnecting layouts on request
  • Ground-floor, step-free access
  • Ideal for families travelling together
