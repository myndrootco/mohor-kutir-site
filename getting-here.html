Getting Here

The road to Mohor Kutir

By train into Bolpur, by road through Ilambazar, or by air into Kolkata — three ways in, all of them leading to the same stretch of the Suri–Bolpur road.

01

Fly Into Kolkata

Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport is Kolkata's main gateway, and the one most guests fly into for Mohor Kutir — about four hours by road from the resort. A smaller regional airport at Andal, Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport, sits closer still, though its flight schedule is limited, so most journeys still begin in Kolkata.

  • Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose Intl. (CCU), Kolkata — about 4 hrs by road
  • Kazi Nazrul Islam Airport, Andal — roughly 55 km, limited flights
  • The front desk can help arrange a car from either airport
02

Arrive By Train, Into Bolpur

Express trains connect Howrah and Sealdah stations in Kolkata to Bolpur Santiniketan in about two and a half to three hours — the easiest way in for most guests. Some services also halt at the smaller Prantik station, a little closer to Santiniketan itself.

  • Bolpur Santiniketan station — Howrah/Sealdah main line, ~2.5–3 hrs from Kolkata
  • Prantik station — a smaller halt, closer to Santiniketan
  • Autos and totos wait outside; the front desk can also send a car to meet you
03

By Road, Through Ilambazar

Mohor Kutir is best reached from Kolkata via Ilambazar and Santiniketan — a drive of about four hours that trades highway for open paddy and sal forest on the last stretch. The resort sits directly on the Suri–Bolpur road, with parking and driver accommodation on site.

  • About 4 hrs from Kolkata, via Ilambazar and Santiniketan
  • On the Suri–Bolpur road, Ballavpur
  • On-site parking, with accommodation for drivers
